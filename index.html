Transfers only run in that direction, and only until the service ends. After that, Pokémon left in Bank stay in Bank.

Get your Pokémon out

  1. Open Pokémon Bank on your 3DS

    Charge the console and connect it to the internet. Bank is no longer available to download, so you need it already installed. Use Poké Transporter first if you still have Pokémon sitting in a game cartridge or Virtual Console title.

  2. Move everything into Pokémon HOME

    Link the same Nintendo Account on both consoles, then move your boxes across. Transferring from Bank needs a Pokémon HOME Premium Plan, and a Pokémon that leaves Bank cannot go back.

  3. Sweep the empty boxes

    Check every Bank box and the Poké Transporter box before you put the 3DS away. Anything still there on 26 February 2027 stops being reachable.
