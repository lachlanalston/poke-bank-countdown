Pokémon Bank questions & answers

Pokémon Bank and Poké Transporter end service on Friday 26 February 2027 at 2:00 pm AEDT. Every answer below is sourced to Nintendo or official Pokémon pages, linked at the bottom.

When does Pokémon Bank shut down?

Friday 26 February 2027 at 2:00 pm AEDT, as Nintendo Australia states it. Nintendo's North American notice gives the same moment as Thursday 25 February 2027 at 7:00 pm PST. Both describe one instant: 03:00 UTC on 26 February 2027.

What happens to Pokémon left in Pokémon Bank?

Nintendo's notice is direct: once the service ends, "transferring Pokémon from Pokémon Bank to Pokémon HOME will also no longer be possible." Anything still sitting in Bank after the deadline stays there, with no route out. Nintendo's instruction is to "move them to Pokémon HOME prior to Pokémon Bank's end of service."

How do I move Pokémon out of Pokémon Bank?

Before you start, you need:

  • A Nintendo 3DS with Pokémon Bank already installed, charged and online
  • Pokémon HOME on a Nintendo Switch
  • The same Nintendo Account linked on both
  • A Pokémon HOME Premium Plan

Then, in order:

  1. If any Pokémon are still in a game rather than in Bank, run Poké Transporter first to bring them into Bank.
  2. Open Pokémon HOME's Nintendo 3DS transfer flow and move your Bank boxes across.
  3. Check every Bank box and the Poké Transporter box before you put the 3DS away.

Do I need a Pokémon HOME Premium Plan?

Yes. The official Pokémon HOME plan comparison lists "Moving Pokémon from Pokémon Bank" as unavailable on the Basic plan and available on the Premium Plan. The free tier cannot receive Bank transfers at all.

Can I move Pokémon back to Pokémon Bank afterwards?

No. Pokémon moved into Pokémon HOME from Pokémon Bank cannot be sent back to Bank, and cannot return to Nintendo 3DS titles. Treat every transfer as permanent and check what you are moving before you confirm.

Is Poké Transporter shutting down too?

Poké Transporter moves Pokémon from older games into Pokémon Bank, so it depends on Bank and stops being useful at the same deadline. Like Bank, it has not been downloadable since 27 March 2023. Finish any Poké Transporter moves well before the end of service, not on the last day.

Can I still download Pokémon Bank?

No. Nintendo states plainly that "Pokémon Bank is not currently available for download." Both Pokémon Bank and Poké Transporter stopped being available on 27 March 2023, when the Nintendo eShop for the Nintendo 3DS family closed. If the app is not already on your system, there is no supported way to get it.

Does Pokémon Bank still cost anything?

No. Since 27 March 2023, Pokémon Bank has been free: paid passes stopped being offered and the service has run at no cost since. The cost that remains is on the other end — the Pokémon HOME Premium Plan needed to receive the transfer.

What is Pokémon Bank?

Pokémon Bank is a Nintendo 3DS application that stores Pokémon online in private boxes, with Poké Transporter as its free companion app for bringing Pokémon in from older games. Pokémon HOME on Nintendo Switch is the service that replaces it.
